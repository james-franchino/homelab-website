Welcome to my home lab

Two servers. Plenty to learn.

My home lab is where I turn my interest in technology into practical experience. I use it to manage files, organize photos, run applications, and explore Linux system administration.

Meet the servers

What is a home lab?

A home lab is a personal setup of computers, software, and networking equipment used for learning and experimentation. It can be as simple as a spare laptop or as extensive as several dedicated servers.

Mine centers on two servers named Destiny and Daedalus. Together, they provide useful services while giving me a place to practice the skills I am studying in college.

Why I built it

I wanted to understand what happens behind the applications I use every day. Running my own services means learning how to install them, configure them, keep them available, and troubleshoot problems when something stops working.

The lab also serves a practical purpose. It helps me store and organize my data while gaining experience relevant to a career in systems administration and cloud operations.
