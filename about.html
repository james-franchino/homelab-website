The person behind the lab

Hi, I'm James.

I'm an Information Technology student at GateWay Community College in Phoenix, Arizona. My focus is Red Hat Linux, and my goal is to work in systems administration and cloud operations.

My education

I'm pursuing an Associate in Applied Science in Information Technology with a specialization in Red Hat Linux. My coursework has included Linux administration, Python programming, and web development.

Returning to school has given me a structured way to develop my technical knowledge. My home lab gives me a place to apply that knowledge outside the classroom.

Why systems administration?

I'm interested in the systems that make applications useful and reliable. Running a service involves more than installing software. It requires understanding storage, networking, permissions, updates, and troubleshooting.

Managing my own servers lets me explore those responsibilities directly. I enjoy having a practical reason to learn how something works and figuring out what to check when it does not.

Skills I'm developing

  • Linux administration and command-line tools
  • Application deployment with Docker and Docker Compose
  • Networking and connectivity troubleshooting
  • Backup planning and data protection
  • System monitoring and log analysis
  • Web development with HTML and CSS

Where I want to go

My goal is to begin a career in systems administration or cloud operations and continue building my skills through professional experience and further education.

This lab is part of that preparation. It gives me systems to maintain, problems to investigate, and experiences I can explain beyond a list of technologies on a resume.

About this website

I created this website for my CIS 133 web development course. It introduces my home lab and shares what I am learning through it.

The site uses HTML to organize its content and a shared CSS stylesheet to provide a consistent layout across all five pages.
